# RouteIQ UAE

## Vision
RouteIQ helps field sales teams in UAE plan customer visits efficiently.

## Phase 1 - MVP

### Features

- Customer List
- Customer Location
- UAE Map
- Search Customer
- Customer Details

### Customer Fields

- Company Name
- Contact Person
- Mobile Number
- Email Address
- Address
- City
- Latitude
- Longitude
- Last Visit Date
- Renewal Date
- Products Owned

## Phase 2

- Nearby Customers
- Route Planning
- Google Maps Navigation

## Phase 3

- Visit Notes
- Renewal Tracking
- Opportunity Management

## Phase 4

- Multi-user SaaS
- Subscription Plans
- Team Management
